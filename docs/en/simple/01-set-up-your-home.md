1️⃣  SET UP YOUR HOME
════════════════════════════════════════

Screen: /setup


📌  WHAT IT DOES

   • Asks a short set of questions about your house
   • There is only ONE home profile
   • You can come back and edit it any time


🕐  WHEN TO USE IT

   • The first time you open the app
   • When your electricity company changes its prices
   • When you get solar panels, a battery, or an electric car


⭐  WHY IT MATTERS

   Two separate reasons.

   1. It narrows down the guessing.
      The meter only reports one total number. When it sees
      a 3000 W jump it has to work out what that was.
      If you said "no electric car, no dryer", those two
      are ruled out straight away.
      Fewer candidates → better guesses.

   2. No prices, no money.
      The breakdown screen always shows energy.
      But the COST column stays blank until you
      enter your prices.


👆  THE STEPS

   Click "Household setup" in the lower part of the
   left sidebar.

   Then fill in four sections.


   📋  Section 1 — "Your home"

      • Home name
        What you call the place, e.g. 12 Smith St
        Up to 80 characters

      • People living here
        A number from 1 to 20

      • Home type
        House · Apartment · Townhouse


   🔥  Section 2 — "Energy systems"

      • Hot water
        Electric · Gas · Solar · Heat pump · Don't know

      • Heating
        Electric · Gas · Reverse-cycle AC · None

      • Cooling
        Split-system AC · Ducted AC · Fans only · None

      💡 "Don't know" is a perfectly good answer.
         Better than guessing wrong — a wrong guess
         sends the system looking for the wrong thing.


   ☀️  Section 3 — "Solar, battery and EV"

      Three yes/no switches.

      • Solar panels — does the house have them?
      • Home battery — is there one storing power?
      • Electric vehicle — is there an electric car?

      💡 These three matter because they make the power
         graph look strange. Solar can push the reading
         NEGATIVE when you sell power back to the grid.
         Charging a car makes a very big, very long step.


   💰  Section 4 — "Electricity tariff"

      Pick one of three kinds.

      • Flat rate
        Same price all day.
        You enter: Rate
        Example: 0.30 per kWh

      • Time of use
        Dearer at busy hours.
        You enter: Peak rate, Off-peak rate
        Example: 0.45 peak / 0.22 off-peak

      • Tiered
        The more you use, the dearer it gets.
        You enter: Tier 1 rate, Tier 1 up to, Tier 2 rate
        Example: 0.25 / 300 kWh / 0.38

      💡 Which one am I on? Look at your bill.
         One price per unit → flat rate.
         Different prices by time of day → time of use.
         Price steps up after so many units → tiered.


   Then press "Save" at the bottom of the page.


✅  WHEN IT'S DONE

   • A small note pops up: "Household saved"
   • The dashboard title changes to your home's name
   • The "Finish setting up your home" prompt disappears
   • On the breakdown screen the cost column fills in


⚠️  WORTH KNOWING

   There's only one home
      This version has no login. One household,
      one shared set of data.

   Time-of-use has two fixed settings
      Peak hours are locked to weekdays 3pm–9pm,
      Australia/Sydney time. The form doesn't ask you
      about this yet. Different time zone or different
      peak hours → the money figures will be off.
      A known limit of this first version.

   Tiered pricing spreads across the whole month
      Tiers apply to your monthly total, not to each
      individual run. So every row on the breakdown
      screen is priced at the month's blended average.


🚫  ERROR MESSAGES YOU MIGHT SEE

   • "Give your home a name"
   • "Whole numbers only"
   • "At least 1 person" / "At most 20 people"
   • "Enter a rate in $/kWh"
   • "A rate can't be negative"
   • "That rate looks too high"
   • "Enter the monthly kWh where tier 2 starts"
